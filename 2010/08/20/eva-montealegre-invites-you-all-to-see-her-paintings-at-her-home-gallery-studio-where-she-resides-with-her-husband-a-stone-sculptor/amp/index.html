Artist Statement

Itâ€s my intention to create a dance of color and form that manifests in a dreamlike conveyance of everything from whimsy to drama.  An energetic flow, a spiritual is-ness and sometimes even an explosion is conveyed.  This intense style of painting allows me to create a tran-substantial truth, an energetic veracity.  My artistic influences include my dear painting instructor, Robert Kingston, most of all, but I have also admired the work of Francis Bacon, Fritz Scholder, Susan Seddon Boulet & Valerie Bechtol.  Sometimes when I am painting I am in a divine state and other times it is more akin to an apache tango dance.
