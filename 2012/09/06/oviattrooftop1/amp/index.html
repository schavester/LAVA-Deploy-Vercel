Penthouse of the Oviatt Building Visit

Kim Cooper
8 years ago

On August 30, 2012, a few lucky subscribers to the LAVA newsletter gathered in the lobby of the art deco Oviatt Building on Olive Street for a very special free private evening tour of the Penthouse from the building’s historian, Marc Chevalier.

If you weren’t fortunate enough to join us on the tour, you will enjoy these photographs of this unique and mysterious place above the city. For more info on this event see lavatransforms.org/oviattrooftop1
